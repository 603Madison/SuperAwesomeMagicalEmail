The first release of SuperAwesomeMagicalEmail: webmail for your family's mailboxes, signed in with
Authentik, with apps for Android and Linux.

## The server and web app

- **Sign in with Authentik** (OpenID Connect), with a group for who may sign in and one for admins.
  A break-glass admin with a password is always there for setting up, and for when Authentik is
  down.
- **Mailboxes you give people:** add a PurelyMail mailbox (or any IMAP and SMTP service) once, with
  its password checked and kept encrypted, and give it to one person or several. People never see
  its password.
- **People before they sign in:** add someone by their Authentik username or email and give them
  their mailboxes; they're waiting for them at their first sign-in.
- **Their own accounts:** people can add Gmail, iCloud, Fastmail, Yahoo or any IMAP account (the
  admin can turn this off).
- **The mail:** every inbox together, each account's folders, search, reading (HTML mail cleaned,
  with images from the web held back until you ask, then fetched through the server), writing,
  replying and forwarding with attachments, drafts, archive, delete, junk, stars, moving, and
  unsubscribing from mailing lists. Keyboard shortcuts, as Gmail's.
- **The family's look:** Lexend, the Aurora, Violet, Ocean and Ember themes, light or dark, and
  bold text, on a computer or a phone.
- One container for Podman or Docker (amd64 and arm64), with a Quadlet and a Compose file.

## SuperAwesomeMagicalEmail for Android

- Enter your server's address and sign in with Authentik in the browser.
- Every inbox, folders, search, reading, writing with attachments, swipe to archive or delete.
- Notifications for new mail, with **Archive** and **Mark read**.
- `mailto:` links and sharing into a new message.

## SuperAwesomeMagicalEmail for Linux

- A native GTK 4 app for Ubuntu 24.04+, Fedora 40+ and SuperAwesomeMagicalLinux
  (`rpm-ostree install` the .rpm).
- Sign in with Authentik in the browser; the mail laid out as the web app, narrowing to one pane
  at a time.
- On SuperAwesomeMagicalLinux it takes the desktop's colours and follows them as they change.
- Notifications for new mail (with Archive and Mark read), and optionally keeps running in the
  background to tell you with the window closed.
- Your `mailto:` handler.
