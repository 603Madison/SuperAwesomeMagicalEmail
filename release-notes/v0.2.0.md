The first published release of SuperAwesomeMagicalEmail: webmail for your family's mailboxes,
signed in with Authentik, with apps for Android and Linux. And sign in once: with
SuperAwesomeMagicalOrchestrator on your phone or computer, Email signs in by itself.

**Security updates since v0.1.0 was written** (v0.1.0 and v0.1.1 were never published, so this
release has everything): the server's web layer (Netty 4.2.20, under Ktor) fixes a critical TLS SNI
bypass and request smuggling and denial-of-service holes; jsoup fixes a gap in how mail's HTML is
cleaned and a memory exhaustion; logback 1.5.38; and the web app's router (React Router 7.18.5)
fixes open redirects.

Everything it's built on is also current: Kotlin 2.4, Ktor 3.6, OkHttp 5, Android Gradle Plugin 9
(Gradle 9.9), the latest AndroidX and Compose, jsoup 1.24, logback 1.6, React 19.3, Vite 8 and
TypeScript 7.

## The server and web app

- **Sign in with Authentik** (OpenID Connect), with a group for who may sign in and one for admins.
  A break-glass admin with a password is always there for setting up, and for when Authentik is
  down.
- **Sign in through SuperAwesomeMagicalOrchestrator:** set its address in **Admin › Server ›
  Orchestrator address** (with a **Test** button), or with `SAME_ORCHESTRATOR_URL`, and list this
  server on the orchestrator as `email`. The apps on a device signed in there are then signed in
  here as the same person. Without an address nothing changes.
  - Whose account is decided as for Authentik: the same person at the same identity provider gets
    the same account, with the same groups and roles, and someone you added ahead is found by
    username or email at their first sign-in this way too. Where the orchestrator and this server
    don't share an identity provider, only an ordinary account with the same username is signed
    in. Never the break-glass admin, never an account that's turned off, and never one still
    waiting for its first single sign-on.
  - Tickets are short-lived, work once, and are checked against the orchestrator's published keys.
    Refusals are logged with the reason and slow down like wrong passwords.
- **Mailboxes you give people:** add a PurelyMail mailbox (or any IMAP and SMTP service) once, with
  its password checked and kept encrypted, and give it to one person or several. People never see
  its password.
- **People before they sign in:** add someone by their Authentik username or email and give them
  their mailboxes; they're waiting for them at their first sign-in.
- **Their own accounts:** people can add Gmail, iCloud, Fastmail, Yahoo or any IMAP account (the
  admin can turn this off).
- **The mail:** every inbox together, each account's folders, search, reading (HTML mail cleaned,
  with images from the web held back until you ask, then fetched through the server), writing,
  replying and forwarding with attachments, drafts, archive, delete, junk, stars, moving, and
  unsubscribing from mailing lists. Keyboard shortcuts, as Gmail's.
- **The family's look:** Lexend, the Aurora, Violet, Ocean and Ember themes, light or dark, and
  bold text, on a computer or a phone.
- One container for Podman or Docker (amd64 and arm64), with a Quadlet and a Compose file.

## SuperAwesomeMagicalEmail for Android

- Enter your server's address and sign in with Authentik in the browser.
- With the SuperAwesomeMagicalOrchestrator app signed in on the phone, Email signs in by itself.
  The first time, the orchestrator asks whether Email may sign in as you, and the sign-in page shows
  **Continue as (you)** with your mail server's address under it, to tap once. Signing out stays
  signed out, until you sign in again. Only the sign-in goes through the orchestrator: never what
  you're reading.
- Every inbox, folders, search, reading, writing with attachments, swipe to archive or delete.
- Notifications for new mail, with **Archive** and **Mark read**.
- `mailto:` links and sharing into a new message.

## SuperAwesomeMagicalEmail for Linux

- A native GTK 4 app for Ubuntu 24.04+, Fedora 40+ and SuperAwesomeMagicalLinux
  (`rpm-ostree install` the .rpm).
- Sign in with Authentik in the browser; or, on SuperAwesomeMagicalLinux (or anywhere the
  orchestrator's agent runs), it signs in as whoever is signed in there, and the sign-in page offers
  **Continue as (you)**. Signing out stays signed out.
- The mail laid out as the web app, narrowing to one pane at a time.
- On SuperAwesomeMagicalLinux it takes the desktop's colours and follows them as they change.
- Notifications for new mail (with Archive and Mark read), and optionally keeps running in the
  background to tell you with the window closed.
- Your `mailto:` handler.
