Sign in once: with SuperAwesomeMagicalOrchestrator on your phone or computer, Email signs in by
itself.

## The server and web app

- **Sign in through SuperAwesomeMagicalOrchestrator:** set its address in **Admin › Server ›
  Orchestrator address** (with a **Test** button), or with `SAME_ORCHESTRATOR_URL`, and list this
  server on the orchestrator as `email`. The apps on a device signed in there are then signed in
  here as the same person. Without an address nothing changes.
- Whose account is decided as for Authentik: the same person at the same identity provider gets the
  same account, with the same groups and roles, and someone you added ahead is found by username or
  email at their first sign-in this way too. Where the orchestrator and this server don't share an
  identity provider, only an ordinary account with the same username is signed in. Never the
  break-glass admin, never an account that's turned off, and never one still waiting for its first
  single sign-on.
- Tickets are short-lived, work once, and are checked against the orchestrator's published keys.
  Refusals are logged with the reason and slow down like wrong passwords.

## SuperAwesomeMagicalEmail for Android

- With the SuperAwesomeMagicalOrchestrator app signed in on the phone, Email signs in by itself.
  The first time, the orchestrator asks whether Email may sign in as you, and the sign-in page shows
  **Continue as (you)** with your mail server's address under it, to tap once.
- Signing out stays signed out, until you sign in again.
- Only the sign-in goes through the orchestrator: never what you're reading.

## SuperAwesomeMagicalEmail for Linux

- On SuperAwesomeMagicalLinux (or anywhere the orchestrator's agent runs), the app signs in as
  whoever is signed in there when it starts, and the sign-in page offers **Continue as (you)**.
- Signing out stays signed out. Nothing new to install, and nothing changes without the
  orchestrator.
